# WeChat Ecosystem Marketing

Use this class-level skill for WeChat official accounts, communities, private-domain funnels, mini-programs, and Weixin Channels.

## Core workflow
1. Define role of each asset: official account for trust/content, group for retention, personal/enterprise WeChat for conversion, mini-program for transaction/service, Channels for video/live reach.
2. Build content cadence: topic pillars, publishing frequency, hooks, visuals, CTA, compliance review.
3. Operate community: entry criteria, rules, rituals, KOC roles, activation, moderation, conversion path.
4. Grow compliantly: avoid induced sharing/follows, spammy mass messaging, sensitive content, deceptive claims.
5. Measure: open rate, share rate, follower net growth, group activity, conversion, LTV, churn/unfollow.

## Subdomains
- **Official accounts**: articles, headlines, layout, 搜一搜, content calendar.
- **Private domain**: lifecycle, segmentation, community-to-1v1 conversion.
- **Weixin Channels**: short video/live stream calendar, public-private linkage.
- **Community operations**: daily/weekly rituals, group roles, retention, soft selling.
