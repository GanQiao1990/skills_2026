# Global Social Platform Marketing

Use this class-level skill for non-China social/community marketing and creator-led growth.

## Core workflow
1. Define audience, market, brand voice, offer, funnel stage, and platform mix.
2. Adapt one core idea into native platform formats instead of cross-posting blindly.
3. Plan cadence: pillars, hooks, formats, engagement rituals, repurposing, measurement.
4. Engage authentically: respond, participate, seed conversation, avoid spam and astroturfing.
5. Measure: reach quality, saves/shares, comments, community growth, leads, conversion, retention.

## Subdomains
- **LinkedIn**: professional thought leadership, founder posts, newsletters, B2B demand.
- **X/Twitter**: fast commentary, threads, reply strategy, network effects.
- **Reddit**: community norms, value-first participation, anti-spam discipline.
- **Instagram/TikTok**: visual storytelling, short-form hooks, creator formats.
- **Podcast/Video/YouTube**: episode strategy, retention, chapters, thumbnails, repurposing.
- **Social strategy/content**: calendars, platform adaptation, brand voice, analytics.
