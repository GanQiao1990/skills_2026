# Legal Business Operations

Use this class-level skill for practical business legal and compliance work. Provide risk-aware drafting support, not formal legal advice.

## Core workflow
1. Identify jurisdiction, document type, parties, business purpose, and risk tolerance.
2. Check authority and enforceability: parties, signatures, approvals, mandatory law, platform rules.
3. Review core obligations: payment, delivery, IP, confidentiality, data, liability, termination, dispute resolution.
4. Classify risks: red-line must-fix, negotiable commercial risk, low-risk note.
5. Provide executable revisions, fallback language, and negotiation rationale.

## Subdomains
- **Contract review**: Chinese Civil Code contract practice, breach, damages, arbitration/litigation, e-signatures.
- **Policy writing**: privacy policy, user agreement, internal policy, data governance, compliance procedures.
- **Business compliance**: PIPL/data, advertising, employment-adjacent policies, audit evidence.
- **Risk summaries**: executive-ready issue/risk/action tables.
