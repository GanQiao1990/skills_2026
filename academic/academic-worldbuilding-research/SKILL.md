# Academic Worldbuilding Research

Use this class-level skill when creative or analytical work needs academically grounded realism.

## Core workflow
1. Locate the setting in time, place, ecology, technology, social structure, and narrative function.
2. Check material constraints: climate, resources, transport, food, institutions, communication, disease, and labor.
3. Build society: kinship, status, power, ritual, belief, economy, law, and conflict.
4. Build people: motivation, cognition, trauma, personality, relationships, incentives.
5. Build story: narrative structure, character arcs, pacing, genre expectations, mythic/folk patterns.
6. Flag anachronism, cultural incoherence, geographic impossibility, and psychological implausibility.

## Subdomains
- **Anthropology**: culture, ritual, kinship, exchange, belief, taboo, ethnographic plausibility.
- **Geography**: terrain, climate, settlement, resources, trade routes, spatial constraints.
- **History**: period accuracy, material culture, historiography, source confidence.
- **Narratology**: plot structure, arcs, tropes, genre mechanics.
- **Psychology**: character motivation, behavior, attachment, trauma, group dynamics.
