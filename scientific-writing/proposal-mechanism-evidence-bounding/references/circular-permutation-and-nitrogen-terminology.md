# Circular permutation and nitrogen terminology notes for proposal writing

## Why this reference exists

Use this note when a proposal draft overstates mechanism certainty around circular permutation / topology rearrangement, or when nitrogen terminology is becoming blurred.

## Evidence boundary for circular permutation

From the FEBS Journal 2024 review "Protein engineering using circular permutation – structure, function, stability, and applications" (Das and Ainavarapu; doi:10.1111/febs.17146):

### Supported by literature
- Circular permutation (CP) is used in applications including:
  - biocatalysis
  - fluorescence-based small-molecule sensing
  - ligand-binding protein switches
  - genome editing variants
  - optogenetic engineering
- CP can preserve the overall fold in many proteins while changing connectivity.
- CP can increase or decrease stability or catalytic efficiency depending on context.
- CP can be useful for biosensor/switch design and ligand-responsive architectures.

### Explicit caution from the literature
- Effects of CP on the protein energy landscape cannot be predicted a priori.
- The outcome is not always predictable and often requires experimental testing.
- Different CP sites can preserve fold but alter folding rate, unfolding rate, ligand binding, catalytic behavior, or stability in very different ways.
- Some examples improve function; others reduce stability or abolish ligand binding.

## Writing consequence for proposal text

Do write:
- "based on a circular-permutation/topology-rearrangement strategy"
- "drawing on prior CP-enabled sensor and switch precedents"
- "to be comparatively evaluated as a candidate dynamic-control module"
- "given the site-dependent effects of CP on stability, binding, and folding"

Do not write:
- "circular permutation will drive the control mechanism"
- "CP establishes a reliable execution mechanism" 
- "CP proves the dynamic module is feasible in this system"

## Nitrogen terminology boundary

### Prefer these distinctions
- Nitrogen uptake:
  transport/import of external nitrogen source
- Nitrogen-source utilization:
  broad use of supplied nitrogen source for productive metabolism
- Nitrogen assimilation:
  biochemical incorporation into central metabolites, typically glutamate/glutamine-centered metabolism
- Nitrogen allocation / redistribution:
  partitioning assimilated nitrogen into biomass, stress response, and target product pathways

### Proposal-writing rule
If the draft refers to use of ammonium salts, amino nitrogen, or other external nitrogen sources, do not automatically label the entire phenomenon as "nitrogen assimilation" unless the claim specifically concerns incorporation through assimilation nodes or enzymes.

Safer wording options:
- "enhanced nitrogen-source utilization"
- "improved nitrogen uptake and assimilation efficiency"
- "improved nitrogen metabolic allocation toward product synthesis"

## Suggested sentence patterns

### Mechanism-bounded CP sentence
"现有研究表明，循环置换可用于构建传感器、蛋白开关和部分配体响应系统，但其对稳定性、配体结合和能量景观的影响具有明显位点依赖性，难以先验预测，因此在本项目中更适宜被界定为可检验的候选动态控制策略，而非预设必然成立的执行机制。"

### Nitrogen terminology sentence
"本项目关注的并非单一外源氮源摄取事件，而是氮源摄取、同化效率及其向目标产物合成方向的分配能否被蛋白层级动态元件有效调节。"
