# Qiao Intelligence Skill

**A comprehensive self-awareness system for understanding your research ecosystem and intellectual identity**

## Overview

This skill provides deep insights into your research portfolio, spanning 100+ projects across computational biology, protein engineering, biomedical data science, and AI/ML infrastructure. It transforms your project landscape into actionable self-knowledge.

## Quick Start

```
"Use the qiao-intelligence skill to analyze my research profile and suggest future directions"
```

## What It Analyzes

### Project Domains
- **Protein Engineering** (40%): CatPredDesign, BoltzDesign1, BindCraft, RFdiffusion2
- **Biomedical Data Science** (25%): UKB_RAP, BLISS, FigureYa, ProteomicCode  
- **Synthetic Biology** (20%): E. coli models, NarQ/NarL, artemisinin biosynthesis
- **AI/ML Infrastructure** (15%): Qwen distillation, autoresearch, Claude skills

### Intellectual Patterns
- Methodological preferences (gradient-based optimization, structure-guided design)
- Knowledge integration style (cross-domain synthesis, iterative refinement)
- Research philosophy (reproducibility, rigor, biological realism)

### Core Competencies
- Expert-level protein design and PyTorch/ML implementation
- Advanced biomedical analysis and scientific computing
- Intermediate metabolic engineering and structural biology

## Key Benefits

1. **Self-Understanding**: Recognize patterns in your research choices and evolution
2. **Strategic Planning**: Identify gaps and opportunities in your expertise
3. **Communication**: Articulate your unique value proposition to collaborators
4. **Decision Making**: Make informed choices about resource allocation
