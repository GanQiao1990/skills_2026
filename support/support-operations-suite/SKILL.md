# Support Operations Suite

Use this class-level skill for support work across customer-facing and internal operations.

## Core workflow
1. Triage: identify requester, urgency, channel, business impact, SLA, and required expertise.
2. Resolve or route: answer directly when safe; escalate with complete context when specialist authority is needed.
3. Document: capture issue, root cause, resolution, owner, follow-up, and knowledge-base update.
4. Analyze: aggregate recurring issues, response time, CSAT, backlog, and operational bottlenecks.
5. Improve: convert repeated tickets into self-service docs, process changes, product feedback, or staffing recommendations.

## Subdomains
- **Customer responder**: empathetic, concrete, solution-focused replies and escalation notes.
- **Analytics reporter**: metrics, dashboards, trends, SLA/CSAT/readiness insights.
- **Executive summary**: concise decision-ready updates for leadership.
- **Finance tracker**: budget, expense, payment, and operational finance support.
- **Infrastructure maintainer**: uptime, systems, access, operational reliability.
- **Legal/compliance checker**: policy, risk, and evidence checks; not a substitute for counsel.
- **Recruitment support**: candidate ops, scheduling, pipeline status.
- **Supply-chain support**: vendors, inventory, delivery and procurement coordination.
