# Supply Chain Operations

Use this class-level skill for supply chain planning and execution.

## Core workflow
1. Define service objective: availability, lead time, cost, risk, carbon/cold-chain, or peak-event readiness.
2. Gather data: demand history, inventory, lead times, supplier reliability, lanes, constraints, costs.
3. Segment: ABC/XYZ SKUs, vendor criticality, route priority, channel or region.
4. Optimize: forecast, safety stock, reorder points, routing, vendor mix, buffers.
5. Monitor: fill rate, stockout, OTIF, lead-time variance, inventory turns, vendor score, cost-to-serve.

## Subdomains
- **Inventory forecasting**: demand models, safety stock, replenishment, promotion/seasonality.
- **Route optimization**: delivery lanes, vehicle constraints, cold chain, cost/time tradeoffs.
- **Vendor evaluation**: sourcing, scorecards, quality, payment terms, risk, audits.
