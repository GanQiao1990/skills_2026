# Finance Operations Strategy Umbrella

Use this as the class-level finance entry point. It unifies analyst, FP&A, forecast, controller, tax, invoice, fraud, and investment workflows.

## Operating principles
- State assumptions before conclusions; every model is a simplified map.
- Separate actuals, estimates, forecasts, and scenarios visibly.
- Use cash flow and working capital as reality checks, not revenue alone.
- Pair recommendations with sensitivity analysis and decision triggers.
- Make outputs audit-ready: source, version, reconciliation, owner, date.

## Core workflow
1. Identify decision: budget, investment, forecast, cash, tax, control, pricing, fraud, or reporting.
2. Gather inputs and reconcile to trusted financial statements or ledgers.
3. Build baseline / upside / downside scenarios and note drivers.
4. Quantify risk, sensitivity, and operational implications.
5. Present an executive recommendation plus action owners.

## Subdomains
- **Financial analysis / FP&A**: variance, KPI dashboards, unit economics, board packs.
- **Forecasting**: revenue, cash runway, burn, hiring, scenario planning.
- **Bookkeeping/controller**: close, reconciliations, chart of accounts, internal controls.
- **Invoices/AP/AR**: three-way match, VAT/sales tax, aging, collections, payment runs.
- **Fraud/risk**: anomaly detection, payment risk, AML/fraud signal triage.
- **Investment research**: diligence, valuation, comps, thesis, portfolio implications.
- **Tax strategy**: compliant optimization, transfer pricing, multi-jurisdiction planning.
