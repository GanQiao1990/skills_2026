# Sales Revenue Operations

Use this as the class-level sales skill.

## Core workflow
1. Establish sales motion: ICP, segment, buyer, pain, urgency, budget, decision process.
2. Qualify: MEDDPICC-style metrics, economic buyer, decision criteria/process, paper process, pain, champion, competition.
3. Plan action: discovery agenda, demo/POC, stakeholder map, mutual action plan, proposal, close plan.
4. Inspect pipeline: stage quality, next step, age, risk, forecast category, expansion potential.
5. Coach and improve: call feedback, objection handling, sequence tests, win/loss insights.

## Subdomains
- **Account strategy**: land-and-expand, stakeholders, QBRs.
- **Discovery coaching**: questions, gap quantification, pain mapping.
- **Deal strategy**: competitive positioning, procurement, close plan.
- **Sales engineering**: technical discovery, demos, POCs, security/architecture responses.
- **Outbound**: ICP signals, personalization, multichannel sequences.
- **Pipeline analytics**: health, velocity, coverage, forecast risk.
- **Proposal/RFP**: win themes, executive summary, compliance matrix.
- **Sales data extraction**: metrics from reports/spreadsheets into dashboards.
